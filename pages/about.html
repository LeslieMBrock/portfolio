A little about the person behind the pixels

Meet the Developer

My journey into programming began with two simple words: "Hello, World!"

That first line of code opened the door to an entirely different universe. Suddenly, imagination could become reality. I discovered a place where I could create something from nothing, give my ideas structure, and watch them come to life.

I learned that technology was more than a tool. It could be a creative partner, helping me explore possibilities, solve problems, and turn ideas into experiences that others could enjoy.

What began with "Hello, World!" grew into a journey through programming, web development, and creative design. Four years later, I'm still discovering what I can bring to life.

☮

01 / The path so far

My Development Journey

My development journey began with a 16-week Code Talk program, where I studied UX design, HTML, CSS, and Agile project management, earning a certificate upon completion. That experience introduced me to both the creative and technical sides of building websites.

I continued my education at Bellevue University, where I'm pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Web Development, with an expected graduation in 2027. My coursework has expanded into JavaScript, C++, programming languages, software architecture, cybersecurity, and artificial intelligence, including generative AI and agentic system architecture.

Beyond the classroom, I've put my education into practice by developing and maintaining a bilingual website. Working with responsive layouts, interactive JavaScript features, and evolving site architecture has taught me the importance of planning, debugging, testing, and writing code that can grow with a project.

Every project brings something new to learn, and that's one of the things I enjoy most about development.

03 / Beyond the browser

Creative Approach

My creative vision is rooted in bringing ideas to life through thoughtful design and functional code. I enjoy combining visual creativity with interactive features to build websites that are engaging, accessible, and purposeful.

Through my development projects, I've explored responsive layouts, interactive experiences, bilingual content, and distinctive visual identities. Each project has challenged me to find a balance between creativity, usability, and maintainability.

I believe a website should do more than look good. It should work well, communicate clearly, and give visitors an experience worth remembering.

04 / A guiding principle

Development Philosophy

"Technology is a creative partner, but humanity must always remain at the heart of what we build."

I believe technology offers extraordinary opportunities to create, connect, and solve problems. Whether I'm writing code, designing an interface, or collaborating with AI, I approach development as a partnership between human imagination and technological possibilities.

That partnership also comes with responsibility. Accessibility, privacy, transparency, and human oversight should be part of the development process, not afterthoughts. I believe good development means understanding not only what technology can do, but how it affects the people who use it.
