04 / Development philosophy

Built for People

"Technology should serve people, not the other way around."
  • Human-centered designStart with people, context, and the task at hand.
  • AccessibilityMake inclusive structure and interaction part of the process.
  • Privacy awarenessConsider personal information and limit unnecessary exposure.
  • Responsible AI assistanceUse AI as an aid, with human review of generated code.
  • Security-conscious developmentLearn and apply careful implementation practices without promising guaranteed security.
  • Clear, maintainable workFavor understandable communication and architecture that can be cared for over time.
